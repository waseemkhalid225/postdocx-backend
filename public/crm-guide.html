FF-CRM — Staff and Owner Guide

The consultancy desk that also does the work

1 · Your door

  1. Staff and owners sign in at foriforeign.com/crm (or your own address /crm/your-name, shown under Team → Your door). The applicant landing never appears there.
  2. Owners open the consultancy at /app#signup-agency; staff join only through the invite link the owner sends, with their own work email. A two-week trial on Starter limits starts automatically; the end date shows on the workspace.
  3. Hard wall: you see your consultancy and your branch only; another consultancy can never see you; ForiForeign's direct applicants are never visible; our staff see your clients masked unless you grant support access for a set number of hours.

2 · Clients and leads

  1. Add a client, import a CSV, or let leads arrive by themselves: your lead email address (leads.your-name@forimail.com), your own WhatsApp number with your own AI key, your website through the API.
  2. Every client gets a ForiForeign address the moment the record is created; replies to it land on the client's screen and in your notifications.
  3. Duplicates are caught by phone or email. Lost clients take a reason; reasons appear in the report. Follow-up sequences nudge silent leads on day 1, 3 and 7 and stop when the stage changes.

3 · Search, prepare, send

  1. Search for a client from their profile: 54 countries, both lanes, verified postings, your own partner universities first (labelled Your partner · P1).
  2. Prepare the full application in the client's name; send from the client's address; every reply is read. Review before send is mandatory: preflight, estimated chance, fraud check.
  3. Bulk: Search for all shown, table view, CSV export. Quota per branch and per person under Team.

4 · Documents and visa desk

  1. The client's vault with six states; expiry alerts; the reader files every upload.
  2. Visa files per route with sourced rules, check-ins from the processing window, decisions read from mail. Status order: mail → your manual update → one automated read after the usual time → you are told to check.
  3. Portal Assist on behalf: from the client's screen, Fetch visa status opens the portal in your browser with the Apply Assistant, you complete the security check, one tap sends the status and screenshot to the client's file.

5 · Your partners

  1. Team → Your partner universities and employers: add or import your agreements (contact, fee, currency, payment days, agreement end, priority). They rank first for your clients; ForiForeign's outreach never approaches them.
  2. Commissions are recorded against the partner and marked received with a date; enrolment evidence (admission number, fee receipt) stays on the client's file.

6 · Finance

  1. Fees and receipts per client; expenses by category and branch; disputes with a reference; bank accounts as records for your invoices.
  2. P&L by branch and by month, clients and enrolments per branch, by consultant, lead sources, sub-agent payables. Your clients pay you; ForiForeign moves none of your money.

7 · Team, branches, white label

  1. Roles: owner, manager, consultant, sub-agent, viewer. Branches and sub-branches with their own quota. Sub-agents see only their own clients.
  2. White label (Growth and Scale): your name, logo, colour, address and domain on every screen and email; staff sign in with their own emails; clients sign up on your domain and belong to you.

8 · Billing

  1. Plans per consultancy, not per user. Reminders at 7, 3 and 0 days; 3 days of grace; renewal through the same checkout; a numbered invoice is emailed on every payment and listed under Billing.
  2. API keys (Growth and Scale) under Team; webhooks for your own systems.

9 · Support

  1. Help in the workspace opens this guide and the FAQs; Write to support creates a ticket routed to your consultancy's owner first and to ForiForeign when you mark it for us. Email: admin@foriforeign.com.

Questions

Can ForiForeign see my clients?

Masked by default. You can grant support access for a set number of hours; it is logged.

Can we add a person who already has a ForiForeign account?

No. FF-CRM is for clients you bring; the platform never links them.

Who collects the client's fees?

You do. FF-CRM records; it never moves your money.

Does the API cost extra?

It comes with Growth and Scale.

Can staff see other branches?

Only owners and managers; consultants and sub-agents see their own scope.

What happens when the plan lapses?

Three days of grace, then the workspace pauses; clients and files are kept.
