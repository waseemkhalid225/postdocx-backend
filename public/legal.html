Terms, Privacy and Consent

Version 2026-09-05 · Draft for legal review · These pages describe exactly what the platform does; they are linked at every consent point in the app.

1. What ForiForeign is and is not

ForiForeign finds, verifies and prepares study and work opportunities, reads and organises your correspondence, and guides you to long-term residence. ForiForeign is not a law firm, not an immigration adviser and not a government authority. Information about visas and residence is drawn from official sources shown next to each rule; it is information, not advice. Where a licensed adviser is required, we say so.

2. You act in your own name

Applications, replies, portal submissions and signatures are yours. When you press Send, the message is sent from your address in your name. When a consultant on the platform acts for you, they do so with your recorded consent and only within the scope you approved; submissions on your behalf are made only by consultants holding a verified registered-agent licence for that country.

3. Your ForiForeign mailbox

At sign-up you receive an address on our domain (for example first.last@forimail.com) that we operate for you. Mail sent to it is stored, read by automated systems to set your next step and prepare replies, and shown to you in the app. You can copy mail to a personal address, pause automated reading, export everything, or close the mailbox at any time. We never create accounts for you with any other provider and never hold a personal password of yours.

4. Portal watch

If you connect an account on a visa centre, immigration or institution portal, we store the password encrypted, sign in as you on the schedule you chose, read the status page, and notify you of changes. Scope is limited to what you approved (watch; upload; submit — submit only with a licensed consultant). A consultant may connect a portal for you; it stays inactive until you confirm. Disconnecting deletes the stored password.

5. Documents

Documents you upload or receive are stored encrypted, read automatically to check type, dates, expiry and consistency, and used only for your applications. Bank statements, visa papers and other confidential items are stored only if you switch that on. Retention: documents are deleted at the retention date shown in your vault; mail after 24 months unless linked to an open case.

6. Payments and refunds

Prices are in USD; local currency is an estimate. Unused case credits are refundable within 14 days; card refunds return by the original method. Agency plans renew monthly by card.

7. Organisations

A consultancy, university, employer or service partner that works with you sees only what your consent and the platform's scope rules allow; organisations cannot see each other's data; platform staff cannot read organisation client lists.

8. Your rights

Download all your data or request deletion from Profile → Language and data. Deletion completes within 30 days unless a legal retention applies. Contact: admin@foriforeign.com.

12. Data processing addendum for organisations

When a consultancy, institution or employer uses ForiForeign, it is the controller of its clients' or applicants' data and ForiForeign is its processor. ForiForeign processes that data only on the organisation's instructions given through the platform, applies the security measures in section 5, keeps a record of processing, assists with data-subject requests through the export and deletion functions, deletes or returns the data at the end of the relationship subject to the retention schedule below, and notifies the organisation of a personal-data breach without undue delay. Sub-processors are listed in section 13; the organisation is told of changes through the platform notice board.

13. Sub-processors

Supabase (database, storage, authentication; region chosen in the platform settings) · Railway (application hosting) · Anthropic, Google and OpenAI (model providers for reading and writing documents; no training on your data under their API terms) · Resend and Cloudflare (email delivery and routing on forimail.com) · Lemon Squeezy or Stripe (payments; they hold card data, we never do) · Meta (WhatsApp Business notifications when enabled) · Google (website translation when the person chooses a language). Each receives only what its function needs.

14. Retention schedule

Active accounts: data kept while the account exists. Closed, rejected or withdrawn cases: generated documents and mail bodies deleted after 180 days; an audit line stays. Inactive accounts: documents and personal fields purged after the retention period set in the platform (default 18 months of inactivity) with notice by email. Payments and invoices: kept for the period tax law requires (Pakistan: six years). Consents: kept for the life of the account plus the statutory limitation period, because they are the record of what you agreed to. Deletion requests: completed within 30 days unless a legal retention applies, and confirmed to you.

15. Cookies and local storage

ForiForeign sets only what the service needs: the session token, your language choice, the dashboard snapshot for speed, and the Google Translate preference when you pick a language. No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers. Because these are strictly necessary, no consent banner is required; you can clear them in your browser at any time.

16. Age

ForiForeign is for people aged 18 or over. A person under 18 may use it only with a parent or guardian who accepts these terms on their behalf and manages the account.

17. Where a licensed adviser is required by law

Some countries restrict who may give immigration advice or lodge applications for a fee (for example Australia's registered migration agents, New Zealand's licensed immigration advisers, Canada's RCICs and the United Kingdom's OISC regime). ForiForeign provides information and preparation; it does not give immigration advice and does not lodge applications for a fee. Where a consultancy on the platform holds such a licence and the applicant has consented, that consultancy may submit on the applicant's behalf; the platform records the licence and the consent.

18. Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of Pakistan. Before any formal step, write to admin@foriforeign.com; most matters are resolved within ten working days. Nothing here limits rights you have under the consumer or data-protection law of the country you live in.

19. Labour recruitment compliance

ForiForeign is not a recruitment agency and does not place workers. For labour, trade, care and seasonal routes the platform shows the official government scheme or employer route, the employer's and the applicant's steps, and the origin-country rule (Pakistan BEOE, India eMigrate, Bangladesh BMET/BOESL, Philippines DMW): use only licensed recruiters and verify the licence. In line with the ILO general principles on fair recruitment, no worker should pay recruitment fees or related costs; where a destination or origin law forbids such fees the platform says so on the route and flags any offer that asks for them. The platform's own charges are for preparation, correspondence and guidance, never for a job, a visa or a permit.

20. No guarantee

ForiForeign shows an estimated chance for each case based on your profile, the posting, verified rules and past outcomes on the platform. It is an estimate, not a promise. No platform, agent or adviser can guarantee an admission, a job, a visa or a permit; a guarantee is a warning sign. Fees paid to ForiForeign are for the work described in the package or plan and are refundable only as set out in section 8.

21. Consultancies, white label and conflicts of interest

ForiForeign serves applicants directly under its own name, and provides its platform to consultancies as their own back-office software under the consultancy's name. To keep the two apart honestly: (a) a consultancy's clients apply under the consultancy's name; the platform's name does not appear on their files to institutions or employers; on the consultancy's own domain the platform's name does not appear in the interface; (b) a consultancy sees only its own clients, branches and members, never ForiForeign's direct applicants or another consultancy's clients; (c) the platform's staff see a consultancy's client list only as counts and stages; names and contact details are masked unless the consultancy grants time-limited support access; ForiForeign staff never contact a consultancy's client with an offer to switch, and a consultancy's client who signs up directly stays attributed to the consultancy for the engagement term recorded on the platform; (d) ForiForeign approaches universities, employers and organisations only under its own name and only for its direct channel; institutions and employers a consultancy declares as its own partners are never approached; ForiForeign takes no share of a consultancy's own commissions; consultancies pay their plan and nothing else; (e) consultancies may not resell access, copy the rule registry, or use the platform to recruit for a competing platform; their plan is per consultancy; (f) matching, scoring and the preflight are identical for every applicant, whichever door they came through; institutions with a countersigned MOU are shown first among the options an applicant qualifies for and are labelled as partners; partner status never makes an applicant eligible; (g) features built for ForiForeign's own business (public destination pages, outreach and proposal agents, acquisition of institutions, cost and economics screens, partner spotlight sales) are not part of the consultancy plans and are not shown on white-label domains.
