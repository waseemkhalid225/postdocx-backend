Questions people ask first
Is searching really free?

Yes. Ten full searches on a free account, three a day, previews included. You pay only when we prepare and send.

Do I send my original documents?

Never. Upload clear scans of attested copies; only the authority you apply to may ask for originals, and it will say so.

Can you guarantee a visa or a job?

No, and nobody honest can. You see an estimated chance with its reasons; anyone promising a result is a warning sign.

Who pays the official fees?

You do, directly to the authority or institution. Our fee covers preparation, correspondence and tracking; we or your consultancy can assist with the steps.

My consultancy already has a CRM.

Import your clients from one CSV; run both for a week; keep the one that does the work.

Where is my data?

Supabase (region set before launch), encrypted identifiers, export and deletion from your profile, the full sub-processor list on the legal page.
