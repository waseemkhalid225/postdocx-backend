The ten modules

What the market calls essential in a study-abroad CRM, and how FF-CRM does each.

1 · Lead capture

Every enquiry lands in one place.

  • Website lead form with your own link and QR
  • WhatsApp enquiries logged to the client
  • Walk-ins and referrals entered in seconds
  • CSV import from any old CRM
  • Lead → client in one tap, assigned to a branch and a consultant

2 · Application tracking, 54 countries

Study, professional and labour lanes, every country's own rules.

  • Per-country requirement checklists (4,800+ sourced rules)
  • Intakes, deadlines, verified postings
  • Real-time stage per client: prepare → sent → reply → offer → visa → enrolled
  • Board and table views, bulk search, filters by branch and consultant

3 · Document vault

Read, not just stored.

  • Every upload read automatically: type, dates, attestation, expiry
  • Six states: required · uploaded · missing · needs correction · ready · used
  • Expiry alerts, version history, role-based visibility
  • Client-side upload portal; never originals

4 · Visa management

A visa desk, not a status field.

  • Six-step files per destination with sourced rules
  • Appointments, tracking references, official tracking links
  • Check-ins from the processing window; decisions read from mail
  • Refusal analysis and resubmission; arrival plan on grant

5 · WhatsApp and email

Under your name, logged to the client.

  • Notifications and reminders on WhatsApp in your name
  • Every application sent from the client's own email address; every reply read and classified
  • Your coordination email as reply-to; client questions routed to you

6 · Tasks and reminders

One next action per client, with a date and an owner.

  • Journey engine sets the next step automatically
  • Overdue flags on the board and table
  • Appointments and deadline alerts

7 · Reports

Numbers the owner can trust.

  • Pipeline by stage, branch and consultant
  • Applications sent, replies, offers, enrolments
  • Quota use per branch and person
  • Export to CSV, logged

8 · Commissions and finance

Yours, recorded.

  • Client finance: fees, receipts, balances
  • Commission ledger per client and institution
  • Enrolment evidence (admission number, fee receipt) on file
  • ForiForeign takes no share of your commissions

9 · Branches and sub-agents

Company-grade structure from day one.

  • Branches and sub-branches with their own quota
  • Roles: owner, manager, consultant, sub-agent, viewer
  • Sub-agents see only their own clients
  • Isolation check you can run yourself

10 · Mobile and white label

Runs on any phone; your brand on your domain.

  • Phone-first screens, bottom sheets, 44-px targets
  • White label: your name, logo, colour, address, contact email, your domain (Growth and Scale)
  • Staff sign in with their own emails
