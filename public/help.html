ForiForeign — Applicant Guide

From your CV to your new life abroad, step by step

1 · Start

  1. Create your account at foriforeign.com → Start free. Choose Study abroad or Work abroad; you can change it any time.
  2. Upload your CV first. Your profile fills itself: degrees with years, experience to the month, skills, languages, tests, licences. Check it under Profile → About me and add what is missing.
  3. Your ForiForeign address (name@forimail.com) is created for you. Every application goes from it and every reply comes back to it. The email inside your CV is never used.

2 · Documents

  1. Profile → Documents is one vault. Drop files or tap the box; each file is read and filed automatically.
  2. Must have for this case lists what the route needs, with instructions (passport bio page, degree certificate not marksheet…). Can strengthen your case lists optional extras.
  3. Highest degree first; files not needed now are marked 'may be asked later'. Nothing is ever sent without your tap.

3 · Find opportunities

  1. Opportunities searches 54 countries in both lanes. Type plain words: 'nurse germany', 'driver job canada', 'scholarship uk'. Typos and regions ('gulf', 'europe') are understood.
  2. Every card shows verified facts: money (in USD and your currency), language needed, pathway (visa route → residence → PR), and for labour jobs the clearance you need before you fly.
  3. Results never come back empty: if nothing exact exists, the search widens and tells you why. Partners with a signed MOU appear first among the options you qualify for, labelled.

4 · Prepare and send

  1. Start case on a card. The case sheet shows a preparation checklist: eligibility, documents, CV, cover, requirements.
  2. Review and send opens the preflight (six checks, estimated chance, employer fraud check). Send goes from your own ForiForeign address, in your own name.
  3. Packages: Start, Smart, Premium. After an offer, one 'plus' payment covers the offer pack, visa desk, interview pack, arrival pack and a year of residence support. Prices show in your currency.

5 · Replies, offers, interviews

  1. Mail opens on what needs you: interview invitations, offers, visa decisions. Every reply is read and classified.
  2. Offers are recorded automatically with their conditions and deadlines; an interview pack is prepared from the posting and your CV.

6 · Visa desk

  1. Open the visa file from the case at offer stage; the route is chosen for you from the sourced rules. Six steps, appointments, tracking reference.
  2. Status order: a decision in your mail counts first; if you check the portal yourself, tap Update status myself; only after the usual processing time does ForiForeign read your connected portal once. It never retries; it tells you to check.
  3. Portal Assist: Connect & fetch status opens the portal in your browser with the Apply Assistant, fills your login, you complete any code or captcha, then one tap sends the status and a screenshot to your file.

7 · After the visa

  1. Arrival plan with dated tasks; family checklist; residence counter and PR pathway under Profile → Options.
  2. Drop the admission letter and fee receipt in the vault: they close the case and start your arrival plan.

8 · Privacy and money

  1. No consultancy can see, add or contact you; your account cannot be attached to any consultancy. You pay ForiForeign only; official fees go directly to the authority.
  2. Export or delete your data from Profile → Settings at any time.

Questions

Is searching free?

Yes. You pay only when a case is prepared and sent.

Which email do universities see?

Your ForiForeign address, in your name. Replies land in Mail.

Can you guarantee a visa?

No one honest can. You see an estimated chance with reasons.

I changed my mind about the lane.

Profile → Options → change lane. Nothing is lost.

A consultant says they manage my case.

Only if you signed up on their website. ForiForeign accounts are never handed to consultancies.
