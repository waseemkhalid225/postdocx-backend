Help and user guide

Answers come from the same FAQ store the platform's support responder uses. Ask anything in the app under "Ask ForiForeign"; if we cannot answer at once, a person replies within 24 hours at admin@foriforeign.com.

Applicants: the journey in one minute

  1. Sign up, upload your CV, complete the short profile.
  2. Search (free) or use "Find in four taps"; open matches and read why each fits.
  3. Choose positions; the platform prepares the application; press Send from your own ForiForeign address.
  4. Replies land in your Mail tab and are read for you; the next step is always on your dashboard.
  5. Offers, interviews, visa desk, arrival plan, family and residence follow the same way.
